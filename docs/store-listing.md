## Full description (≤4000 chars)
Sofra is your family's shared table — a warm, simple meal planner that helps you decide
what's for dinner, remember what you actually cook, and stop repeating the same dishes on autopilot.

Log any meal in seconds — home-cooked, takeout, or dining out — with one or many dishes and
per-dish star ratings. Sofra quietly learns your kitchen and turns it into a weekly meal plan
and insights you'll actually use.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Plan your week — personalized weekly meal plans built from your own cooking history.
• Log any meal fast — home, takeout, or restaurant, with multiple dishes and ratings.
• Kids' tiffin planning — plan children's school lunches separately from family meals.
• Grocery list — pull ingredients straight from your plan and tick them off as you shop.
• Recipes & notes — keep a recipe link or steps and personal notes on any dish.
• 1,400+ starter dishes — an Indian-first catalog spanning regional and world cuisines.
• See your patterns — most-cooked dishes, dishes overdue for a comeback, and a dine-out budget.
• Restaurant memory — remember which dishes to order (and which to skip) at each place.
• Share with family — one household, everyone in sync; share dish packs with friends.
• Gentle reminders — so tomorrow's menu is never a surprise.
• Calm light & dark themes, readable for all ages.

WHY SOFRA
The daily "what should we cook?" is exhausting. Sofra remembers for you — what you made,
what everyone liked, what's overdue — so meal decisions get easier every week. No calorie
counting, no clutter. Just a family meal planner that fits real life.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
Meals sync privately to your household, encrypted in transit. You can delete your account
and all data anytime from Settings.
