# Sofra — 10 Brand Commercial Prompts (20s each)

Real-people shoots, warm and honest — never techy. Palette on set: terracotta, sage, warm
cream, natural wood, real food. Music: warm acoustic, gentle. Every spot ends on the Sofra
logo lockup + "Your family's meal memory" + a Google Play badge (~2s end-card).
Each brief lists: **Cast · Setting · Beats (with timecodes) · App screen · Line/Super**.

---

### 1. "The 6 PM Blank Stare"
**Cast:** One tired parent. **Setting:** Home kitchen, golden-hour light, open fridge.
**Beats:** 0–6s parent stares blankly into a full fridge, sighs. 7–12s pulls out phone, opens
Sofra Home → *"Dishes you haven't made in a while."* 13–18s face lights up, grabs ingredients,
pan hits stove. 19–20s end-card.
**Screen:** Home (forgotten-dish list). **Super:** "Your kitchen already knew."

### 2. "Sunday Reset"
**Cast:** Family of four at the table, Sunday morning. **Setting:** Sunlit dining table, coffee, cereal.
**Beats:** 0–7s cheerful chaos, "what are we eating this week?" 8–13s a parent taps **Generate** —
the week fills in, day by day. 14–18s kids peek, confetti burst on accept, high-five. 19–20s end-card.
**Screen:** Plan → celebration. **Super:** "A week, planned in one tap."

### 3. "Never Forgotten"
**Cast:** Grandmother + adult child cooking together. **Setting:** Rustic kitchen, handwritten recipe card.
**Beats:** 0–8s grandmother shows a dish, child tastes, wide eyes — logs it in Sofra. 9–15s time-jump
(seasons change, same kitchen). 16–19s months later, Sofra surfaces the dish; child cooks it for their
own kids. 19–20s end-card. **Screen:** Add meal → Home. **Super:** "Recipes your family never loses."

### 4. "Two Homes, One Kitchen"
**Cast:** A couple, split-screen in two locations. **Setting:** One at a market, one at home.
**Beats:** 0–6s partner A adds groceries-to-meal on the go. 7–12s partner B at home sees it appear
instantly on the shared household. 13–18s they cook it together that night. 19–20s end-card.
**Screen:** Household / Home (shared). **Super:** "One shared table, wherever you are."

### 5. "Where Did It Go?"
**Cast:** A couple reviewing the month. **Setting:** Couch, takeout containers subtly in frame.
**Beats:** 0–7s "we didn't eat out *that* much… right?" 8–13s open Insights → Home vs Outside bar +
spend. 14–18s knowing laugh, resolve to cook more; quick cut to a home-cooked meal. 19–20s end-card.
**Screen:** Insights (Home vs Outside, spend). **Super:** "See where the money really goes."

### 6. "Tiffin Morning"
**Cast:** Parent + child, school morning. **Setting:** Kitchen, lunchbox open on counter.
**Beats:** 0–6s child: "not the same lunch again." 7–12s parent checks Sofra kids-tiffin — variety
suggestion. 13–18s packs something new, child grins, runs to bus. 19–20s end-card.
**Screen:** Kids tiffin (Home/Plan). **Super:** "Beat lunchbox déjà vu."

### 7. "Ten Seconds"
**Cast:** Anyone, post-dinner. **Setting:** Kitchen, plates being cleared. Fast, kinetic cuts.
**Beats:** 0–4s dinner ends. 5–15s snappy montage: tap +, pick dish, rate it, done — logged in ~10s,
clock overlay. 16–19s phone set down, lights off. 19–20s end-card. **Screen:** Add meal.
**Super:** "Logged in ten seconds. Remembered forever."

### 8. "Last Time We Were Here"
**Cast:** Two friends / a couple at a restaurant. **Setting:** Cozy restaurant table, menus up.
**Beats:** 0–7s "what did we get last time?" 8–13s open Sofra restaurant memory — the dishes they
loved (and the one they didn't). 14–18s confident order, waiter nods. 19–20s end-card.
**Screen:** Insights Top Restaurants / restaurant detail. **Super:** "Order like a regular. Everywhere."

### 9. "The Honest Mirror"
**Cast:** Solo, reflective. **Setting:** Quiet kitchen, end of month, warm lamp.
**Beats:** 0–8s narration-free; person scrolls Insights. 9–15s home-cooked ratio climbs on screen,
small proud smile. 16–19s pours a glass, satisfied. 19–20s end-card. **Screen:** Insights.
**Super:** "Small habits. Honest numbers."

### 10. "Share the Win"
**Cast:** One person + reactions from a family group chat. **Setting:** Kitchen, then quick phone-screen inserts.
**Beats:** 0–7s finishes a great week of home cooking. 8–13s taps Share → a branded Sofra stat card.
14–18s family group chat lights up with 🔥❤️👏. 19–20s end-card. **Screen:** Share card.
**Super:** "Cooked it. Flexed it."

## Production notes
- **Format:** shoot vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) for Shorts/Reels; a 16:9 cutdown for YouTube pre-roll.
- **App inserts:** use the branded screenshot frames in `branding/assets/sofra_shot_*.png`, or
  screen-record the live app for authenticity (recapture with clean data first).
- **Consistent end-card:** `sofra_lockup.png` on cream, tagline, Google Play badge. Hold ~2s.
- **Casting:** keep it globally relatable — the app is not region-specific; vary households across spots.
- **Food styling:** real, everyday home meals (pasta, stir-fry, roast, tacos), not staged gourmet.
